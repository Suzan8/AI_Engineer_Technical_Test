# Return and Refund Policy

Customers may return products within **30 days** of delivery.

Requirements:

- Product must be unused.
- Original packaging is required.
- Original receipt must be provided.

Refunds are processed within **7 business days** after the returned item passes inspection.

Products damaged by misuse are not eligible for refund.

Customized products cannot be returned unless defective.

Shipping fees are non-refundable.
